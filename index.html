Gamelan, made of earth

Gamelan is the traditional ensemble music of Java, usually played on bronze or iron. Gamelan Keramik plays on ceramic instead. The sound is softer and rounder, and every blade is formed and fired by hand.

The name stands for two things at once: the workshop that makes the instruments, and the group of players who bring them to life.

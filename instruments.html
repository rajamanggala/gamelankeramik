Words you may meet

Gamelan
A traditional ensemble of Java and Bali, made mostly of tuned percussion.
Bilah (blade)
The struck bar that makes one note. Here, made of ceramic.
Slendro
A five-note tuning system used in gamelan.
Pelog
A seven-note tuning system used in gamelan.

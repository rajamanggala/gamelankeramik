Why does a ceramic blade ring at a certain pitch?

Science · 12 November 2026 · 6 min read

Placeholder text. When a blade is struck it vibrates, and the speed of that vibration is the pitch we hear. A shorter or thicker blade vibrates faster, so it sounds higher.

Length, thickness and material

Placeholder text. The stiffness of the fired clay also matters, which is why ceramic sounds different from bronze or iron even when the blade has the same shape.

The Group

Four players, one name, one sound made of clay.

One name, two roles

Gamelan Keramik began as a workshop that makes gamelan instruments from ceramic. The musicians who play them took the same name, so the workshop and the group are one identity.

Our playing is calm, elegant and carefully prepared, for audiences of every age who love music and the arts.
